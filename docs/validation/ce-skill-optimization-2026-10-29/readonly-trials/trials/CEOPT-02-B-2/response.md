The product scope is aligned: support agents get one **Copy reference** action beside the existing ticket reference, helping them respond to customers quickly.

It copies exactly the displayed public reference, excluding the customer’s name, email, and internal ticket ID. Keyboard users can reach and activate it. A successful copy shows a brief confirmation; clipboard failure shows a clear message and allows retry, without claiming success.

Bulk copy, export, settings, and analytics remain out of scope. This completes the chat-only alignment; no further product choices are needed.

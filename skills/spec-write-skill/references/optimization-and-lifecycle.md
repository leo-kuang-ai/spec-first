# Optimization And Feedback Handoff

**trigger_condition:** primary intent is measurable optimization, or field feedback/transcript/issue received. **must_read:** Must read completely before evaluating `spec-optimize` handoff or feedback-to-regression. **fallback_if_unread:** Handle as a regular revise; do not claim that the handoff optimization is complete or that feedback allows entry into regression. **eval_case:** Triggered by `primary-metric-optimization-handoff` and `feedback-unreproduced-observation`; not triggered by ordinary source revise.

When no authoring patch is present and the primary goal is measured optimization, maintain the existing near-neighbor behavior: `base_operation=null`, `effect=not-entered`, and hand off to `spec-optimize`. Standard and clear revisions shall still be completed solely by the designated owner of this workflow; the authoring → optimization → authoring cycle is strictly prohibited.

handoff must carry at least the following: source snapshot, mutable/immutablescope, trigger evidence, baseline, protected behavior, treatment, controlled variables, metric, budget, stop condition, rollback, invalidation, and owner-contract limitations. Currently, `spec-optimize` cannot persistently express treatment arms, paired comparisons, or rollback/invalidation; in such cases, closeout can only specify `execution_mode=manual_observation` and `not promotable`; this is a non-persistent prose label and must not be written to the `spec-optimize` YAML under `execution.mode`, nor should any side-channel experiment database/runner be established.

feedback must be categorized first by route, behavior, tool, target, or project failure. Becomes a regression only after minimal de-identification reproduction, user confirmation of expected behavior, and authorization of eval-source mutation; feedback that cannot be reproduced remains as observation. Retain reviewable diff upon post-write evaluation or payload smoke failure; block completion assertion, provide preview of correction or rollback; do not automatically promote to durable knowledge.

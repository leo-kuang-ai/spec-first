# R29/R30 Multi-Round Decisions and Fix Verification

Load for round 2+ with a complete decision primer. `document-intake.md` owns reuse freshness. A summary alone cannot authorize suppression or prove a fix landed.

## R29 Settled decisions

Match the current consequence and remedy against each prior finding and its retained constituents. Section/title normalization and evidence overlap locate candidates; they do not decide equivalence. Suppress the same issue already settled by Skip, Defer, Acknowledge or a user-settled Withdraw, retaining the actual decision and reason. Do not revive it as FYI, residual risk or a deferred question.

Re-evaluate when relevant source, constraints, commitments or scope materially change, even if the old quote remains verbatim. Explain the changed basis instead of presenting a renamed duplicate as new. A section rename alone does not create a new defect.

An Apply-triggered withdrawal remains provisional until the actual edit resolves its premise. It is not a rejected-class decision; failed or unknown Apply restores the dependent for inspection. Record suppressed settled duplicates in Coverage.

## R30 Verify Applied findings

Re-read the actual document and relevant source. Verify whether the intended correction resolves the original consequence, including merged constituents and dependent withdrawals. Similar quotes do not prove failure; different quotes do not prove success.

If the original defect remains, record `fix did not land` with prior finding identity and current evidence. If resolved, record verification in Coverage, not as a new finding or residual. A genuinely distinct defect follows normal consequence admission. Unknown writes remain unverified until readback; do not repeat an edit merely because its tool result was lost.
